How to choose

  • Want one simple score? Look at score-based tools like Danelfin or AltIndex.
  • Want analyst and insider data? Look at TipRanks.
  • Want to see whether AI models agree? That's what we built QuantCall AI for. See multi-model AI stock analysis.
  • Want to screen for ideas? Compare the free limits on WallStreetZen and Intellectia AI.

What to check in any AI stock tool

  • How recent is its data, and does it say?
  • Does it explain why, or just hand you a score?
  • Does it show the bear case?
  • Does it make performance claims? If so, can you verify them?
